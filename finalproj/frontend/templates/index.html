UVA Occupancy Safety Monitor

Estimate building occupancy from mobile-device counts and compare it against fire-code limits.

This dashboard treats detected mobile devices as a proxy for people in an area. Review historical captures by building, inspect the latest location snapshots, and use the estimated occupancy signal to decide when a space may be approaching capacity.
